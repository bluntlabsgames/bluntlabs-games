Taxi Out! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what the game Taxi Out! (the "Game") does with information. It is published by Blunt Labs Private Limited, Safilguda, Secunderabad, Hyderabad, Telangana, India ("we", "us"). You can reach us at support@fabricdost.com.

The short version

  • The Game has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your progress (level, coins, settings) is saved on your phone only.
  • The Game is for players aged 13 and over.
  • The Game shows ads and sells optional in-game items. These are provided by Google (AdMob and Google Play). Their handling of data is described below.

Age check

When you first open the Game we ask for your birth month and year. We use the answer once, to decide whether you may play and which kind of ads you see. We do not store your birth date. We only remember the result (for example "adult" or "teen"). If you are under 13 the Game will not let you play.

Information on your phone

The Game stores your progress, coins, purchases you have made, settings (sound, music, vibration) and the day you installed it, in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not receive this information. Deleting the app's data or uninstalling the Game removes it.

Ads

The Game shows ads served by Google AdMob.

  • Players aged 18 and over may see ads personalised by Google, which can use your device's advertising ID and information about how you use apps. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Players aged 13 to 17 (and anyone whose age we cannot confirm) are shown ads that are not personalised. We ask Google to treat these ad requests as child-directed and to show only family-appropriate ads.

Google's use of data is described in Google's advertising policies and Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Optional purchases (for example "Remove Ads" or coin packs) are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We receive a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase happened so we can give you the item. Coins and items in the Game are virtual, have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.

Game updates

The Game can download updates to its content (levels, text, images) from our update server. When your phone contacts that server, the server can see your IP address and basic technical details, as with any web request. We use this only to deliver the update and do not use it to identify you.

Anonymous statistics

The Game may count simple things such as how many levels were played or which screens were opened, so we can improve it. These are totals only. They contain no user ID, device ID, advertising ID, name, email or location, and cannot be linked to you.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not collect precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not build profiles about you.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is normally nothing for us to delete or correct. You can clear the Game's data in your phone settings at any time. For questions or requests about privacy, email support@fabricdost.com. If you believe a child under 13 is playing, please contact us and we will help.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the Game.
